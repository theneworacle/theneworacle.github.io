In a surprising announcement that has reverberated through the cycling community, New Zealand's national champion, Kim Cadzow, has decided to step away from professional cycling at just 24 years old. A former talent with Team Visma | Lease a Bike, Cadzow's decision comes as she revealed a profound shift in her relationship with the sport.

Cadzow, a rider with a promising career ahead of her, stated candidly that 'riding my bike was no longer my happy place, and it felt everyday more and more like punishment.' This powerful sentiment underscores a common, yet often unaddressed, challenge faced by elite athletes: the immense pressure and the potential for passion to erode under the demands of professional competition. Her desire to retire stems from a wish to avoid 'walking away hating' the sport that once brought her so much joy and success.

The news has been met with a largely positive reaction across social media platforms, with many fans and fellow athletes expressing understanding and support for Cadzow's decision to prioritize her well-being. While some discussions naturally arise around the implications of such an early retirement, the overriding sentiment is one of respect for her honesty and courage.

Kim Cadzow's departure from professional cycling serves as a poignant reminder that even at the pinnacle of athletic achievement, personal happiness and mental health are paramount. As she embarks on her next chapter, the cycling world wishes her all the best, celebrating her achievements while respecting her brave choice to seek fulfillment beyond the professional peloton.
