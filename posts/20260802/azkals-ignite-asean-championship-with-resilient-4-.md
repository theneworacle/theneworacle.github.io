The Philippine national football team, the Azkals, have made a statement in the 2026 ASEAN Championship, securing a hard-fought 4-1 victory over Laos. The match, played in Vientiane, saw Coach Carles Cuadrat's side demonstrate impressive resilience, turning an early deficit into a commanding win.

The game began with an unexpected jolt for the Azkals, as Laos managed to break through and take an early lead, sending a ripple of concern through Filipino supporters. However, the team quickly regrouped, showcasing their tactical discipline and attacking prowess. A powerful second-half rally saw the Philippines net four unanswered goals, effectively shutting down Laos's challenge and putting themselves firmly in contention in the tournament.

This crucial first win provides a significant boost for the Azkals as they navigate the challenging group stages of the ASEAN Championship. The performance highlighted both areas for improvement and the undeniable fighting spirit of the squad.

On social media, the victory has been met with largely positive reactions from fans, celebrating the team's comeback and the vital points gained. While some debates and discussions surrounding game specifics have emerged, the overall sentiment is one of pride and optimism for the Azkals' journey in the championship. With this initial hurdle cleared, the team will be looking to build on this momentum in their upcoming fixtures.
