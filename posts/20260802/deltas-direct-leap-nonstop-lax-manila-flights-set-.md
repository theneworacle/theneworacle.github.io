Travelers looking to fly directly from the U.S. mainland to the Philippines will soon have a new option as Delta Airlines announced its inaugural nonstop service from Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) to Manila (MNL), set to commence on March 28, 2027.

This new route signifies a major strategic move for Delta, reinforcing its commitment to expanding its transpacific network. The airline will utilize its advanced Airbus A350-900 aircraft for this service, promising a premium travel experience for passengers.

Delta's chief commercial officer, Joe Esposito, highlighted that transpacific routes represent a substantial growth opportunity for the airline, driven by high customer demand for destinations not previously served directly. This initiative follows recent expansions from Delta's LAX hub, including new flights to Hong Kong and Melbourne, Australia, further solidifying its presence in the Pacific region.

While Delta has historically served Manila via layovers, often through Seoul or Tokyo, the upcoming LAX-Manila route will be its first direct connection between the U.S. mainland and the Philippine capital. This makes Delta the second airline, and notably the first U.S. carrier, to offer nonstop flights on this popular corridor. Currently, Philippine Airlines operates a direct service, and United Airlines also provides flights to Manila from its San Francisco hub, offering travelers an expanding array of choices.

The announcement has been met with enthusiasm, particularly from the Philippine Department of Tourism (DOT), which anticipates a significant boost in tourism and enhanced connectivity between the two nations. Initial social media sentiment has also been largely positive, with travelers expressing excitement for the increased convenience and options.
