Artificial intelligence is rapidly transforming various sectors, and now it's making its presence felt in the realm of public grievances. A recent surge in AI-generated complaints is creating an unprecedented workload for public bodies such as local councils, schools, and ombudsmen, pushing their resources to the brink.

Since the widespread availability of tools like ChatGPT, public-facing staff have observed a dramatic increase in the volume and complexity of complaints. What once might have been a single-page grievance about an unemptied bin can now become a multi-page document, meticulously crafted by AI, citing regulations, acts of Parliament, and even case law. These elaborate submissions often require extensive review by staff, and sometimes legal consultation, significantly driving up administrative costs and operational strain.

While this digital deluge presents significant challenges, it also offers a silver lining. For many, AI is democratizing the complaint process, making it more accessible and effective. Individuals who struggle with written communication, such as those who are neurodivergent, or those intimidated by traditional bureaucratic processes, find AI chatbots invaluable in articulating their concerns. Social media is rife with examples of people successfully using AI to challenge parking fines or appeal benefits decisions, highlighting its potential to empower citizens.

However, this newfound accessibility comes with its own set of problems. Experts note an increase in trivial or unjustified complaints, which, when amplified by AI's ability to generate lengthy and complex arguments, further exacerbates the workload. One significant issue is the potential for AI to misquote legislation or make factual errors, demanding even more time from public servants to clarify and correct information.

Public sector workers acknowledge AI's role in breaking down barriers to challenging decisions. The core dilemma lies in preserving this benefit while simultaneously discouraging frivolous requests and managing the sheer volume and intricacy of AI-assisted complaints. Proposed solutions include the implementation of standardized forms to limit complaint length, and ironically, the public bodies themselves leveraging AI to help process and respond to the influx of digital grievances.

As AI continues to evolve, its thoughtful and responsible integration into public services will be crucial. The challenge ahead is to harness its power for improved accessibility without allowing it to overwhelm the very systems designed to serve the public.
