The White House is taking a proactive stance on Artificial Intelligence regulation, recently holding meetings with major AI developers, including Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI. These discussions center around a proposed new framework that would see AI companies voluntarily submit their frontier models to the government for review before their public or business launch.

This initiative precedes what is anticipated to be a significant push for comprehensive AI regulation. The engagement reflects the ongoing debate in Washington regarding the future of AI governance, aiming to strike a balance between fostering innovation and ensuring responsible development.

Initial social sentiment surrounding the White House's efforts on AI regulation appears largely positive, though discussions and debates on the specifics of such frameworks are ongoing.
