VietJet, the Vietnamese low-cost carrier, is set to significantly enhance its presence in the Philippines with the launch of two new direct routes to Cebu. Beginning November 2026, the airline will connect Cebu with both Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, operating three weekly services on each route using Airbus A321 aircraft. This expansion triples VietJet's network in the archipelago, adding to its existing Ho Chi Minh City-Manila service.

The decision to prioritize Cebu underscores the city's burgeoning importance as an economic, tourism, and aviation hub. Market intelligence data reveals a compelling narrative of escalating demand: two-way passenger traffic between Vietnam and the Philippines surged by an impressive 41.1% in 2025 compared to the previous year, reaching approximately 1.06 million passengers. While Manila remains a key destination, Cebu has experienced a phenomenal growth in traffic, with passenger numbers increasing by 189%. Notably, the Ho Chi Minh City-Cebu route witnessed a staggering 287% rise in passenger volume in 2025 alone.

This strategic expansion is underpinned by VietJet's robust financial health. The airline reported a stellar first half of 2026, with consolidated revenue climbing 44% year-over-year to reach VND51.536 trillion (approximately $1.96 billion). Consolidated after-tax profit also saw a significant increase, hitting VND1.372 trillion. During this period, VietJet successfully operated 72,000 flights, transporting over 13.4 million passengers and nearly 41,000 metric tons of cargo.

VietJet's broader vision extends beyond this latest Philippine expansion. The carrier's network now boasts 213 routes, with a strong emphasis on international services (167 routes). The airline continues to invest heavily in its future, maintaining a substantial order book of over 600 Airbus and Boeing aircraft, positioning itself for sustained global network development towards 2030. This aggressive growth strategy and strong financial performance have garnered generally positive social media sentiment, indicating a favorable reception to VietJet's ongoing expansion and strategic initiatives.

By strengthening its connectivity to the Philippines, VietJet is not only catering to increasing travel demand but also facilitating greater cultural and economic exchange between Vietnam and the Philippines, and indeed, across the wider Southeast Asian region.
