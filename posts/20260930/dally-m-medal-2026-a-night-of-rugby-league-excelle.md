Sydney was the focal point for the highly anticipated 2026 Dally M Medal Awards, a prestigious event held on the eve of the NRL and NRLW Grand Finals. The ceremony was set to be a dazzling recognition of outstanding talent and dedication in the world of rugby league, bringing together players, coaches, and fans to celebrate a remarkable season.

Injured Panthers skipper Nathan Cleary emerged as a strong favourite for the coveted NRL Dally M Medal, even as he recovered from a recent leg injury that required surgery. His exceptional performance throughout the season clearly left a lasting impression on voters. In the NRLW, Broncos superstar Tamika Upton was a leading contender, aiming to secure a consecutive Dally M Medal, a testament to her consistent brilliance on the field.

The Dally M Awards encompass a wide array of categories, honouring excellence across various positions and roles. These would include positional awards such as Fullback, Wingers, Centres, Five-Eighth, Halfback, Hooker, Props, Backrowers, and Lock of the Year for both the NRL and NRLW competitions. Additionally, significant accolades like Coach of the Year, Captain of the Year, and Rookie of the Year were set to be presented, highlighting leadership, strategic prowess, and emerging talent.

Beyond individual and team recognitions, the ceremony was also set to celebrate other noteworthy achievements with awards like the Provan-Summons Medal, Ken Stephen Medal, Ken Irvine Medal, and Veronica White Medal. Excitement also built around the NRL and NRLW Try of the Year, Tackle of the Year, Top Point Scorer, and the Peter Frilingos Headline Moment of the Year, showcasing the most thrilling and impactful moments of the season.

Several players were noted for their strong performances in specific areas, including Josh Addo-Carr (Eels), Jamal Fogarty (Sea Eagles), Campbell Graham (Rabbitohs), James Tedesco (Roosters), Tom Jenkins (Panthers - 30 tries), Alex Johnston (Rabbitohs - 30 tries), Jamayne Isaako (Dolphins - 310 points), Jesse Southwell (Broncos - 114 points), and Jessica Sergis (Roosters - 17 tries).

Social media sentiment surrounding the Dally M Medal 2026 was largely positive, reflecting the excitement and anticipation of fans. While some debates and differing opinions were observed, the overall atmosphere was one of celebration for the athletes and their incredible achievements. As the event unfolded, the complete list of confirmed winners for all categories was eagerly awaited, promising further insights into the standout performers of the year.
