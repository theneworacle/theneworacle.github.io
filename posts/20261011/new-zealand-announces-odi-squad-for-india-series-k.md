New Zealand has unveiled its One-Day International (ODI) squad for the upcoming five-match series against India, set to commence on November 4 at Eden Park in Auckland. The squad sees the return of several prominent players, including Finn Allen, Lockie Ferguson, and Tim Seifert, with Rachin Ravindra also rejoining the team after recovering from a shoulder injury. Mitchell Santner has been named to captain the side.

The series will follow a five-match T20I series scheduled from October 22 to November 1. Notably, New Zealand has adopted a split squad approach for the ODI series. A core group of players will participate in the first three matches, while seven additional players will join for the fourth and fifth ODIs. This strategic approach is in preparation for an upcoming Test series against India, with some team members transitioning to Wellington after the third ODI to begin Test preparations.

Finn Allen and Lockie Ferguson last played an ODI in 2023, with Ferguson's most recent appearance being in the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup semi-final against India in Mumbai. Tim Seifert's last ODI outing was approximately 18 months ago during a home series against Pakistan.

The squad for the first three ODIs includes Mitchell Santner (C), Michael Bracewell, Mark Chapman, Devon Conway, Jacob Duffy, Matt Henry, Kyle Jamieson, Tom Latham, Daryl Mitchell, Glenn Phillips, Rachin Ravindra, Nathan Smith, Will Young, and Blair Tickner. For the final two ODIs, Santner will be joined by Finn Allen, Michael Bracewell, Mark Chapman, Kristian Clarke, Jacob Duffy, Matt Fisher, Lockie Ferguson, Mitch Hay (WK), Nick Kelly, Tim Seifert (WK), Blair Tickner, and Will Young. The 14-member Test squad for the India series is expected to be announced in early November.

This selection aims to build on New Zealand's historic 2-1 ODI series victory over India in January, which marked their first ODI series win against India on Indian soil.
