The vibrant animation scene in Oregon is getting a significant boost as Portland State University students gain invaluable, behind-the-scenes stop-motion training directly from the acclaimed Laika Studios. This exciting collaboration highlights a strong commitment to fostering local talent and advancing the art of stop-motion animation.

While the specific details of the training program remain a closely guarded secret, the initiative signals a promising future for aspiring animators in the region. Laika Studios, known for its meticulously crafted films like 'Coraline,' 'ParaNorman,' and 'Kubo and the Two Strings,' is a powerhouse in the animation industry and a major local employer.

The timing of this training is particularly relevant as Laika is actively working on its highly anticipated new stop-motion feature, 'Wildwood.' Set right in Portland, this film further solidifies the studio's connection to the local community and its dedication to telling Oregon-centric stories. The anticipation surrounding 'Wildwood' is immense, with early trailers showcasing Laika's signature blend of intricate craftsmanship and compelling storytelling.

This partnership not only offers students unparalleled practical experience but also strengthens the bond between academic institutions and leading creative industries. The enthusiasm surrounding the 'Portland State Laika Studios stop-motion training' is largely positive on social media, reflecting the community's excitement for this unique educational opportunity and the future of animation in the Pacific Northwest.
