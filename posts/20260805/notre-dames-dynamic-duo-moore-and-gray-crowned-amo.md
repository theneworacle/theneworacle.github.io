As the 2026 college football season approaches, all eyes are on the players poised to make a significant impact. ESPN recently released its list of the top-40 most important players, and two Notre Dame stars have firmly cemented their place: cornerbacks Leonard Moore and Christian Gray.

ESPN's Bill Connelly specifically lauded Moore and Gray at No. 24, grouping them under the banner of 'pure transcendence potential.' Connelly didn't shy away from praising their combined prowess, remarking, 'You rarely see a cornerback duo this ridiculous at the college level… But as a duo, they're unfair. You'd better not ever have to pass on Notre Dame.' This highlights the immense advantage their lockdown capabilities will provide to the Irish defense this fall.

Interestingly, while the spotlight shines brightly on Moore and Gray, other notable Notre Dame talents were also discussed. Quarterback CJ Carr, for instance, was excluded from the list, primarily because his impressive performances last year have already established him as a 'known commodity.' Meanwhile, running back Aneyas Williams and wide receiver Micah Gilbert were identified as 'potential stars in need of a breakthrough,' suggesting exciting prospects for the future.

The recognition of Moore and Gray underscores the elite star power within the Notre Dame roster, with many players expected to contend for All-American honors and national awards. Social media reaction to ESPN's selections has been largely positive, with fans and analysts alike engaging in healthy debate about the choices, further fueling anticipation for the season ahead.
