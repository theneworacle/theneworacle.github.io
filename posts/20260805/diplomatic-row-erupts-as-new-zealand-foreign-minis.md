A fiery exchange in the New Zealand Parliament has escalated into a significant diplomatic incident between New Zealand and China. Foreign Minister Winston Peters is facing strong criticism and an official diplomatic protest from Beijing after remarks he made to Chinese-born Member of Parliament, Lawrence Xu-Nan.

The controversy began when Peters reportedly told Xu-Nan to "go back to your own country" during a parliamentary debate. This comment quickly drew accusations of racism and has since broadened into a serious diplomatic issue.

China has formally protested the remarks, highlighting the gravity with which the incident is being viewed internationally. Despite the backlash and the diplomatic intervention, Foreign Minister Peters has, so far, refused to retract his statement.

The incident has not only sparked a political debate within New Zealand but has also garnered significant attention online. While social media sentiment is simulated as 'mostly positive', there is also a considerable amount of debate surrounding Peters' comments and their implications for race relations and international diplomacy.
