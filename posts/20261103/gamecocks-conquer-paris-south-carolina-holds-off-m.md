The much-anticipated women's college basketball season kicked off with a bang in Paris, France, as the top-ranked South Carolina Gamecocks faced off against the No. 20 Maryland Terrapins. In a game that delivered both early dominance and a thrilling comeback attempt, South Carolina ultimately prevailed with a 101-93 victory.

From the opening whistle, South Carolina asserted their authority, quickly building a substantial lead. Their offensive prowess was on full display, racking up 27 fast-break points and capitalizing on 21 points off turnovers in the first half alone. Joyce Edwards led the charge for the Gamecocks, finishing with an impressive 23 points, demonstrating why her team holds the top spot.

However, the Terrapins were not to be outdone. After trailing significantly at halftime, Maryland unleashed a furious second-half rally, spearheaded by an unforgettable performance from Addi Mack. Mack exploded for a career-high 35 points, with an astounding 31 of those coming in the second half, bringing Maryland within striking distance and forcing a crucial timeout from South Carolina coach Dawn Staley.

The Gamecocks, showing championship mettle, regrouped after the timeout and managed to fend off Maryland's relentless pursuit, securing the hard-fought win. The international setting also offered a special moment for South Carolina's French players, Alicia Tournabize and Justine Loubens, who had the unique opportunity to play their season opener in their home country.

The game was a spectacle of high-scoring basketball, dominant runs, and remarkable individual performances, leaving fans eager for what the rest of the season holds for both teams. Social media sentiment largely reflected the excitement, with many praising the dramatic swings and the competitive spirit displayed.
