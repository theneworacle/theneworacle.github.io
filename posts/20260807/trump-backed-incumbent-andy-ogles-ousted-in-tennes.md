In a notable upset in Tennessee's political landscape, Republican incumbent Representative Andy Ogles has been defeated in the primary election for the 5th Congressional District. Ogles, who had received an endorsement from former President Donald Trump, lost to former Tennessee Agriculture Commissioner Charlie Hatcher. The outcome marks a significant moment, especially given that Ogles was the first Trump-backed House Republican to lose a primary in this cycle.

The race saw Tennessee Governor Bill Lee publicly backing Hatcher, setting up a clash within the Republican party. Ogles had recently drawn criticism for controversial anti-Muslim remarks, which may have played a role in the primary results. 

Social media reaction to Ogles' loss has been predominantly positive, though some debate around the outcome and its implications is also evident. This defeat highlights the complexities of endorsements and public sentiment within the current political climate.
