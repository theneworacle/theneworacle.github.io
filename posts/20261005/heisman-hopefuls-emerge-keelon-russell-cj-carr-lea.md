The race for the Heisman Trophy is heating up, and two new names have firmly planted themselves among the top contenders: Keelon Russell, quarterback for the Alabama Crimson Tide, and CJ Carr, quarterback for the Notre Dame Fighting Irish. Both signal-callers delivered standout performances in Week 5, earning them a coveted spot on the Sporting News' Heisman Watch list.

Keelon Russell's Heisman stock is soaring after a dominant display against Mississippi State. Russell was instrumental in Alabama's decisive 56-23 victory, throwing for three touchdowns and adding another on the ground. His season statistics underscore his impact, boasting a 71.2% completion rate, 1,414 passing yards, and 11 touchdowns through five games. College football analysts and fans alike have lauded Russell's poised and prolific play, with social media sentiment overwhelmingly positive, recognizing him as a legitimate threat for the prestigious award.

Not to be outdone, Notre Dame's CJ Carr also made a compelling case for Heisman consideration. Carr led his team to a victory over North Carolina, showcasing his arm talent with four touchdown passes. His season highlights include a 72.4% completion rate, 1,197 passing yards, and 13 touchdowns, complemented by three rushing touchdowns.

While Russell and Carr are ascending, the Heisman landscape is dynamic. Other key players currently in strong contention include Trinidad Chambliss (Ole Miss), Josh Hoover (Indiana), Darian Mensah (Miami), and explosive wide receiver Jeremiah Smith (Ohio State). Smith, in particular, has been making history with his receiving numbers, proving that a non-quarterback can still be a serious threat for the award.

Conversely, some notable names have slipped into the 'fringe' category. Quarterbacks like Bear Bachmeier (BYU) and Texas' Arch Manning, along with running back Jadan Baugh (Florida) and receiver Malachi Toney (Miami), saw their Heisman momentum wane after less impactful recent performances. However, new faces are emerging on the fringe, such as Missouri's Jamal Roberts, who had a monster rushing day, and Nebraska's Anthony Colandrea, who impressed with both his arm and legs.

The overall social sentiment surrounding Keelon Russell and CJ Carr's Heisman candidacies is largely positive, with ongoing discussions and healthy debate among college football enthusiasts and experts about who will ultimately hoist the iconic trophy.
