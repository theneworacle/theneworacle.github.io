The Philippines is taking a significant step to bolster its burgeoning film industry with the revamp of its International Co-Production Fund (ICOF). Unveiled by the Film Philippines Office (FPO) at the prestigious Busan International Film Festival's Asian Contents & Film Market, the new framework introduces a two-track system designed to incentivize international collaborations. Under this innovative structure, the financial support provided through the ICOF will now be directly tied to the size of the Filipino stake in a film project. This strategic move is anticipated to not only encourage more global partnerships but also to significantly boost local participation and growth within the Philippine cinematic landscape. Initial reactions to this development on social media have been largely positive, with discussions highlighting the potential for increased investment and international exposure for Filipino filmmakers.
