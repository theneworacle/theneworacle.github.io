A diplomatic incident at the recent Seoul Defence Dialogue has brought the simmering tensions in the South China Sea back into sharp focus. Philippine Defence Secretary Gilberto C. Teodoro Jr. publicly shamed China after a private note from a Chinese military attaché was delivered to him mid-speech.

During his address, Secretary Teodoro paused to read the contents of the unsolicited note aloud to the international audience. The document, which China later confirmed to reflect its consistent position, challenged the legitimacy of the 2016 arbitration ruling by the Permanent Court of Arbitration, a decision that invalidated China's expansive claims in the South China Sea under its 'nine-dash line' and upheld the Philippines' sovereign rights.

In a powerful rebuke, Secretary Teodoro lambasted China's methods as a display of "coercion, aggression, and outright disrespect of any diplomatic nicety, basic decency and good manners." He declared his intention to keep the note as a "souvenir to demonstrate Beijing's weakness in an international forum like this," underscoring Manila's unwavering commitment to international law and the 2016 UNCLOS ruling.

China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs responded by accusing the Philippines of "instigating trouble and undermining bilateral ties," reiterating Beijing's dismissal of the arbitration decision. This exchange highlights the irreconcilable divides and persistent territorial disputes that continue to strain Philippines-China relations.

This incident at the Seoul Defence Dialogue not only showcased the Philippines' firm resolve in defending its sovereign territory but also garnered significant attention, with simulated social sentiment largely positive towards Teodoro's decisive stand, albeit with some ongoing debate.
