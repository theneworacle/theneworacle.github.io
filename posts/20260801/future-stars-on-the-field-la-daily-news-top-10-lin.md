Linebackers are the undisputed tone-setters of any high school football team. They are the explosive tacklers, the turnover-forcers, the sack-deliverers, and the havoc-wreakers. As the 2026 season approaches, the excitement is palpable as scouts and fans eagerly anticipate which defensive maestros will rise to prominence.

Today, the LA Daily News has unveiled its highly anticipated list of the Top 10 linebackers to watch in 2026, highlighting the most promising high school talents across the Los Angeles area. This comprehensive list features Division I prospects, rising underclassmen, and key contenders from the LA City Section, all poised to make a significant impact on the gridiron.

Here's a closer look at the future stars who made the cut:

*   **Aidan Dagher (Burbank, Senior):** Known for his ferocious blitzing, Dagher is a force off the snap, consistently breaking through blocks to make tackles in the backfield. His relentless pursuit of the football was evident with 112 tackles, 24 tackles for loss, 8.5 sacks, and nine quarterback hurries. Dagher has already garnered significant college attention, holding offers from Louisville, Southern Methodist, Colorado State, Sacramento State, Campbell, and Western Kentucky.

*   **Josiah Rand (Chaminade, Senior):** With elite speed and an uncanny nose for the football, Rand tied for his team's lead with 50 tackles. He also contributed two pass deflections and forced a fumble. His exceptional speed allows him to excel as both a middle linebacker and, at times, drop back into coverage as a safety. Rand is committed to Washington State.

*   **George Hastings (Agoura, Senior):** Hastings has consistently proven himself as one of the state's top players. He is a tenacious finisher of tackles, bringing a grit and toughness that promises to elevate Agoura's performance in 2026. Last season, Hastings recorded an impressive 130 tackles, intercepted a pass, and deflected four passes while forcing one fumble. Beyond his on-field prowess, Hastings is also an academic standout, boasting a 1520 on the SAT, with offers from Johns Hopkins and Tufts.

*   **Hayden Kennington (Oaks Christian, Senior):** Kennington emerged as a crucial leader for Oaks Christian, contributing 73 tackles, a tackle for loss, two fumble recoveries, and a forced fumble. His 5-foot-9 frame belies his toughness and edge, making him a vital component of an already formidable defense.

*   **Ivan Gonzalez (Birmingham, Senior):** An elite pursuer of the football, Gonzalez was an anchor for Birmingham's defense, generating 72 tackles, including four for loss. His efforts were instrumental in a defense that held opponents to under 10 points on four separate occasions last season.

*   **Eli Zamorano (Sierra Canyon, Sophomore):** A rising sophomore with an elite pedigree (his father, Tony, was a CIF All-City linebacker in 1994), Zamorano is considered one of the top recruits in the 2029 class. The 5-foot-11 talent already holds multiple offers, including from UCLA.

*   **Darryl Jones (Crespi, Junior):** Jones made a significant impact as a pass rusher, leveraging his 5-foot-9 stature to his advantage. His tackling fundamentals are described as elite, allowing him to attack hips, drive arms, and chop feet to consistently force ball carriers backward. He proved his high-level play last season with 63 tackles, five tackles for loss, and three sacks.

*   **Justin Simon (Valencia, Junior):** Simon effectively patrolled the short field for Valencia in 2025, delivering crucial tackles. His impressive 6-foot-2 length, combined with his speed and footwork, played a key role in the Vikings' 9-2 season and their Foothill League title. Simon aims to build on his sophomore success, which included 25 tackles.

*   **Grayer Giuliani-Gibbons (Grace, Senior):** Giuliani-Gibbons had a breakout junior year, amassing 130 tackles, forcing two fumbles, and contributing 3.5 tackles for loss. His stellar performance helped Grace rebound from its post-COVID era challenges to achieve an 11-4 record and capture the Citrus Coast title.

*   **Matthew Farias (Granada Hills, Senior):** While perhaps not from a big-name school, Farias is a player with undeniable ability and physical intangibles. His hands and skill in manhandling blockers made him one of the best defensive players in the West Valley League. Farias recorded 121 tackles, five pass deflections, and two interceptions, positioning Granada Hills for major strides in 2026.

Social media reaction to the list has been largely positive, with some lively debate surrounding the individual rankings—a testament to the passion of high school football fans in Los Angeles. While other news outlets have published more general college football linebacker rankings for 2026, this LA Daily News list provides a vital spotlight on the local talent that will shape the future of the sport.

These ten athletes represent the pinnacle of high school linebacker talent in the LA area, and their journeys will be closely watched as they continue to develop and potentially move on to collegiate and professional careers.
