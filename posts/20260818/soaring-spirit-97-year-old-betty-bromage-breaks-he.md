Age is just a number for Betty Bromage, the indomitable 97-year-old British woman who has soared into the record books once more, breaking her own world record as the oldest female wing walker. This awe-inspiring feat is made even more remarkable by the fact that it comes just a year after she suffered a stroke, showcasing an incredible spirit of resilience and determination.

The Cheltenham resident, already a veteran of the skies, strapped herself to a biplane, demonstrating a courage that belies her years. Her previous record-setting wing walk was an achievement in itself, but to return to the wing so soon after a significant health challenge truly sets her apart.

The news of Betty's accomplishment has been met with widespread admiration and a largely positive sentiment across social platforms. While some discussions naturally touch upon the inherent risks of such an endeavor, the overwhelming public reaction is one of celebration for her adventurous spirit and inspiring tenacity. Betty Bromage serves as a powerful reminder that passion and courage know no age limits, encouraging us all to embrace life's adventures, no matter our stage.
