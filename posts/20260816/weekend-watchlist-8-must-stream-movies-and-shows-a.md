As summer gently winds down, streaming services are turning up the heat with an impressive array of new releases. Whether you're looking for a laugh, an adrenaline rush, a heartwarming story, or a deep dive into music, this weekend (August 14-16) has something for everyone across your favorite platforms.

Here's a closer look at the 8 must-watch movies and shows hitting your screens:

### The Invite

Director Olivia Wilde leads this acclaimed comedy, co-starring Seth Rogen. This social comedy, riddled with anxiety and absurd humor, follows a struggling married couple whose dinner party with their enigmatic upstairs neighbors quickly spirals out of control. Originally a theatrical release in early July, you can now rent or buy "The Invite" on-demand this week.

### Reacher Season 4 (Prime Video)

The highly anticipated fourth season of Prime Video's action-heavy thriller is back! Based on Lee Child's novel "Gone Tomorrow," this season sees Alan Ritchson's Jack Reacher embroiled in a complex and deadly game after a chance subway encounter goes awry. The first three episodes are streaming now, promising plenty of intense action for fans.

### Tires Season 3 (Netflix)

Shane Gillis's popular workplace comedy returns to Netflix with a complete third season. Follow Shane Jacobs (Gillis) and Will Jacobs (Steve Gerben) as they team up once again to try and save their family's struggling auto-repair shop. Expect more sarcasm-laden laughs that fans have come to love.

### Camp Rock 3 (Disney+)

The sequel Disney Channel fans never thought they'd see is here! "Camp Rock 3" premieres, with the Jonas Brothers reprising their roles as Connect 3. They return to Camp Rock to host a competition to find the opening act for their next national tour, bringing back beloved characters and fostering new friendships.

### My Brilliant Career (Netflix)

This six-part Netflix series is a spirited, heartfelt adaptation of Miles Franklin's novel. Set in early 20th-century rural Australia, it follows a young girl (Phillippa Northeast) with dreams of becoming a writer, whose aspirations are challenged by an unexpected romance. Fans of YA period dramas like "The Buccaneers" will enjoy this.

### Travis Barker: Louder Than Fear (Hulu)

Music documentary enthusiasts will want to check out this Hulu original. This doc explores not only Blink-182 drummer Travis Barker's prolific music career but also his profound struggles with grief, pain, and survival, particularly in the aftermath of the devastating plane crash that nearly took his life.

### Don’t Say Good Luck (Netflix)

One of the few scripted movies premiering directly on streaming this week, "Don’t Say Good Luck" is a heartwarming film from director Julia Hart. It follows theater kid Sophia (Sunny Sandler) as she prepares for her high school musical, only for sudden family drama to turn her life upside down. Featuring an all-star cast including Melanie Lynskey and Steve Buscemi, this could be an understated hit.

### Lanterns (HBO Max)

Get ready for a new chapter in the DC Universe with HBO's "Lanterns." From co-creators Chris Mundy, Damon Lindelof, and Tom King, the series stars Kyle Chandler as veteran Green Lantern Hal Jordan and Aaron Pierre as new recruit John Stewart. They are dispatched to a small Midwest town to investigate a murder connected to a much wider cosmic mystery, promising to be an integral and potentially blockbuster piece of the DC Universe.

With such a diverse slate of new content, your weekend watchlist just got a whole lot more exciting. Grab your favorite snacks, settle in, and enjoy the cinematic journey!
