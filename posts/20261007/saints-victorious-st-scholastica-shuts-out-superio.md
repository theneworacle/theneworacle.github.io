DULUTH, Minn. – In a spirited Twin Ports soccer showdown, St. Scholastica emerged victorious, defeating the University of Wisconsin-Superior with a clean 2-0 sheet. The match, held in Duluth, Minnesota, saw the Saints deliver a commanding performance.

The winning effort was spearheaded by Captain Lily Rippie, who netted the Saints' crucial first goal. The defense was equally formidable, with St. Scholastica's goalkeeper, Chloe Patrick, making a series of critical saves throughout the game to deny Superior any scoring opportunities and secure the shutout.

The Twin Ports rivalry always brings an extra layer of excitement, and this matchup was no exception. The Saints' cohesive teamwork and strategic play ultimately sealed their impressive win. Social sentiment following the game has been largely positive, reflecting the excitement and passion surrounding local college sports.

This victory marks a significant moment for St. Scholastica, showcasing their strength and determination on the soccer field and further fueling the competitive spirit of Twin Ports athletics.
