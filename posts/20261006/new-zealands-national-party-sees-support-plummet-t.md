Recent political polling in New Zealand has sent ripples through the ruling National Party, with a new RNZ-Reid Research poll indicating a significant drop in public support. The party's backing has fallen to 25.9%, marking its weakest level since the 2020 general election.

In contrast, the opposition Green Party appears to be gaining momentum, with its support climbing to 14.8%. This shift in voter sentiment highlights a changing political landscape in Aotearoa.

Despite these challenging figures, Prime Minister Christopher Luxon has affirmed his leadership, publicly stating his intention not to resign. This declaration comes amidst growing scrutiny of the party's direction and public appeal.
