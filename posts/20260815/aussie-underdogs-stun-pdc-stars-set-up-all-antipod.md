The SkyCity New Zealand Darts Masters kicked off with a bang in Auckland, as two Australian underdogs, Raymond Smith and Adam Leek, sent shockwaves through the competition by knocking out established PDC stars and advancing to the quarter-finals. Their remarkable performances have set the stage for an exciting all-Antipodean showdown.

Raymond Smith, making a triumphant return to the PDC stage after an eight-year hiatus, delivered a masterful display against third seed Stephen Bunting. Smith dominated the match with a superb 6-2 victory, boasting an impressive 99.81 average and converting 86% of his doubles. He sealed his win with a clinical 124 checkout, leaving no doubt about his intent.

Adam Leek, on his World Series debut, proved his mettle in a nail-biting encounter against Ross Smith. Trailing 4-1, Leek mounted a sensational comeback, winning five of the final six legs to clinch a dramatic last-leg decider. His resilience under pressure captivated the audience and secured his unexpected progression.

These stunning victories mean Smith and Leek will now face each other in an eagerly anticipated quarter-final match, guaranteeing an Australian presence in the semi-finals. The opening night also saw top seed Gian van Veen secure a 6-1 win against Jonny Tata, while Gerwyn Price whitewashed Mark Cleaver with a convincing 6-1 scoreline. Damon Heta also fought back to defeat Ben Robb.

The quarter-finals, semi-finals, and final are all scheduled for Saturday at Spark Arena, promising a thrilling conclusion to the New Zealand Darts Masters. The event has already generated considerable buzz, with social media sentiment largely positive, reflecting the excitement and surprise sparked by the Australian contingent's success.
