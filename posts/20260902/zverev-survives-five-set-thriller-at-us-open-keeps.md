In a display of sheer resilience, Alexander Zverev, the newly crowned top seed at the US Open, clawed his way back from the brink of defeat to secure a hard-fought victory against Italian Lorenzo Sonego. The German star triumphed in a thrilling five-set epic, with a final score of 6-4, 3-6, 6-7, 7-5, 6-4, after being down two sets to one. His ability to grind out the win in the early hours of Wednesday morning in Queens has set a dramatic tone for his tournament campaign.

Zverev's elevated status as the No. 1 seed comes in a tournament already marked by significant shifts. Top-ranked Jannik Sinner is sidelined due to a knee injury, and the tennis world was stunned by Novak Djokovic's unexpected first-round exit. These developments open a clearer path for Zverev, offering him a prime opportunity not only to clinch his maiden US Open title but also to potentially ascend to the coveted world No. 1 ranking for the first time in his career. Meanwhile, seven-time major champion Carlos Alcaraz is also making his return to the court, defending his New York title after a four-month absence due due to a wrist injury, adding another layer of intrigue to the men's draw.

Looking ahead, Zverev is set to face Frenchman Quentin Halys in the Round of 64 on Thursday, a match he'll undoubtedly approach with renewed confidence after his gritty opening performance. On social media, fans have largely reacted positively to Zverev's dramatic win, with discussions buzzing around his comeback and the unfolding dynamics of this year's unpredictable US Open.
